\section{Introduction}\label{sec:introduction}

\subsection{Purpose and Scope}
This document describes the backend software architecture of \textit{Trade2}, a mobile application designed to help retired elderly people combat social isolation and loss of purpose by connecting them with neighbours to exchange skills and knowledge.

The need for such an application is supported by concerning demographic and health trends. The United Nations projects that the global population aged 65 and older will more than double from approximately 800 million in 2025 to 1.7 billion by 2050 \cite{un-population}. Simultaneously, research indicates that retirement can lead to reduced social contact, loss of purpose, and increased risk of depression \cite{who2023, dang2023}. Furthermore, untreated depression in older adults is linked to higher risks of chronic illnesses, cognitive decline, and premature mortality \cite{chapman2004}. Trade2 addresses these challenges by facilitating meaningful social connections and providing a sense of purpose through skill-sharing within local communities.

The document follows the C4 model and details the architectural decisions made during the design and development of the backend system. This documentation covers the backend architecture of Trade2, including its system context, container structure, component design, deployment strategy, and testing approach. The scope is limited to the backend software architecture and specifically focuses on the \textbf{Robustness \& Availability} advanced track, which encompasses zero-downtime deployment, comprehensive monitoring and logging, and data consistency through transaction management.

\subsection{System Context}
The Trade2 application enables users to share and request skills within their local neighbourhood, fostering community engagement and meaningful social interaction. The system supports core functionalities including user registration, profile management, skill-sharing listings (offers and requests), location-based discovery, and real-time messaging between users.

\Cref{fig:c1} illustrates the system context, showing how users interact with the Trade2 mobile application and how the backend system interfaces with external services including authentication, geolocation, file storage, push notifications, and the monitoring/logging infrastructure that supports the Robustness \& Availability advanced track requirements.

The backend architecture prioritizes simplicity for maintainability, scalability to support growing user bases, security for data protection, high availability through redundancy and monitoring, and comprehensive observability through logging and metrics collection. These design principles ensure the system can reliably serve elderly users who depend on consistent and accessible service.
