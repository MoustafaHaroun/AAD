\section{Test Cases}\label{sec:hd-testplan-test-cases}

Each test case below states the requirement(s) it verifies, its pre-condition, the steps to execute it, and its expected result.

\begin{htable}[caption = {TC001}, label = {tc:001}, presep = 1em, postsep = 2em]{colspec={lX}}
    Test Case ID & TC001 \\
    Name & Register a new account with valid data \\
    Description & A new user successfully creates an account through the registration wizard. \\
    Requirements & FR1 \\
    Pre-condition & The app is installed and the user is on the Landing screen; the email address used is not already registered. \\
    Steps & 1) Tap Register. 2) Enter a unique email and a password meeting the policy (min. 8 characters, incl. lowercase, uppercase, digit, and special character), and confirm it. 3) Continue and enter first/last name. 4) Continue and enter address fields. 5) Continue and optionally skip the profile photo step. 6) Tap Create account. \\
    End condition & The account exists and the user is signed in. \\
    Expected result & The app signs the new user in automatically and navigates to the Home screen. \\
    Result & PASSED \\
\end{htable}

\begin{htable}[caption = {TC002}, label = {tc:002}, postsep = 2em]{colspec={lX}}
    Test Case ID & TC002 \\
    Name & Register with an email already in use \\
    Description & Registration is rejected when the email address is already registered. \\
    Requirements & FR1 \\
    Pre-condition & An account already exists with the email address used in this test (see TC001). \\
    Steps & 1) Start registration. 2) Enter the same email used in TC001. 3) Complete the remaining steps and submit. \\
    End condition & No new account is created. \\
    Expected result & The app shows an error and the user remains on the registration flow (the backend returns 409 Conflict). \\
    Result & Not run \\
\end{htable}
