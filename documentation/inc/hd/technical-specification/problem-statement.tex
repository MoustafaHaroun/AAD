\section{Problem Statement}\label{sec:hd-problem-statement}

\subsection{What is the app?}\label{sec:hd-what-is-the-app}
The Trade2 application enables users to share and request skills within their local neighbourhood, fostering community engagement and meaningful social interaction.
The app supports user registration, profile management, skill-sharing listings (offers and requests), location-based discovery of listings, and real-time messaging between users.

\subsection{Problem}\label{sec:hd-problem}
The United Nations projects that the global population aged 65 and older will more than double from approximately 800 million in 2025 to 1.7 billion by 2050 \cite{un-population}.
Research indicates that retirement can lead to reduced social contact, loss of purpose, and increased risk of depression \cite{who2023, dang2023}.
Furthermore, untreated depression in older adults is linked to higher risks of chronic illnesses, cognitive decline, and premature mortality \cite{chapman2004}.

\subsection{Why is this app needed?}\label{sec:hd-why-needed}
Trade2 addresses these challenges by facilitating meaningful social connections and providing a sense of purpose through skill-sharing within local communities.
The mobile app is the primary way elderly users reach the platform, so its design prioritizes usability, accessibility, and reliability for this user group.
