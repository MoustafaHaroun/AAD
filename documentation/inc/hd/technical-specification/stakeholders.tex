\section{Stakeholders}\label{sec:hd-stakeholders}
The following stakeholders have been identified as relevant to the Trade2 system, as shown in \cref{tab:hd-stakeholders}.

\begin{htable}[
    caption = {Stakeholders of the Trade2 system},
    label = {tab:hd-stakeholders},
]{
    colspec={lX},
}
    Stakeholder & Interest \\
    End users & The primary users of the application. They rely on Trade2 to find and share skills within their neighbourhood, and have a direct interest in the usability, accessibility, and reliability of the mobile app. \\
    Development team & Responsible for the design and development of the front-end. They define the design decisions documented in this report. \\
    System administrators & Responsible for monitoring system health, analyzing logs, and ensuring high availability of the application. \\
\end{htable}
