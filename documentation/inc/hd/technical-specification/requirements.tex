\section{Requirements}
\label{sec:requirements}

The \tradesq{} Mobile Application must satisfy the functional and non-functional requirements in this section.
The requirements are derived from the Design Essentials user requirements, the Back-end technical specification, and the requirements of Hybrid Development Rubrics.

\subsection{Prioritisation}
\label{sec:prioritisation}

Requirements are prioritised using the MoSCoW method.
Must requirements are required for an acceptable final application release.
Should requirements are important and are implemented unless a documented technical limitation prevents their inclusion.
Could requirements are valuable enhancements that may be omitted without preventing the core application from operating.
Won't requirements are intentionally excluded from the current project scope.
