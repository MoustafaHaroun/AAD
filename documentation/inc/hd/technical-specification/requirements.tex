\section{Requirements}\label{sec:hd-requirements}
The requirements below are adapted from the backend's functional/non-functional requirements (\cref{sec:technical-requirements} of the Technical Specification), rephrased from the mobile app's perspective. IDs and priorities are kept consistent with the backend document for traceability.

\subsection{Functional Requirements}\label{sec:hd-functional-requirements}

\begin{htable}[
    caption = {Front-end Functional Requirements},
    label = {tab:hd-functional-requirements},
]
    {
        colspec={lXl},
    }
        Id & Requirement & MoSCoW \\
        FR1 & Allow a user to register and log in & Must \\
        FR2 & Allow a user to create and manage their profile & Must \\
        FR3 & Allow a user to create skill listings for offers and requests & Must \\
        FR4 & Allow a user to edit and delete their listings & Must \\
        FR5 & Show listings based on the user's location & Could \\
        FR6 & Allow a user to express interest in a listing & Must \\
        FR7 & Allow a user to search listings by skill & Should \\
        FR8 & Allow a user to save and view favorite listings & Could \\
        FR9 & Provide real-time or near-real-time messaging between users & Could \\
        FR10 & Allow a user to filter listings by criteria & Must \\
        FR11 & Allow a user to upload images for their profile and listings & Must \\
        FR12 & Show notifications for messages and listing interest & Should \\
\end{htable}
